About Us

Welcome to SGIP

Shri Ganpati Institute of Pharmacy is committed to providing quality pharmacy education in Kannupur, Jalalpur, Ambedkar Nagar, Uttar Pradesh. Affiliated with the Uttar Pradesh Board of Technical Education (UPBTE), Lucknow, the institute offers D.Pharm and B.Pharm programs that combine strong theoretical foundations with practical laboratory training.

Our campus provides modern classrooms, well-equipped pharmaceutical laboratories, library facilities, and a supportive learning environment. Practical sessions, seminars, academic activities, sports, and cultural programs help students connect classroom learning with the professional responsibilities of pharmacy.

Our experienced faculty and administrative team work together to develop students with scientific knowledge, practical skills, discipline, confidence, and professional values for careers in healthcare and the pharmaceutical industry.

Vision & Mission

The values that guide our teaching, student support, and contribution to healthcare.

Our Vision

To be a trusted centre for pharmacy education that develops knowledgeable, ethical, and confident professionals who improve healthcare and serve society with compassion.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, practical, and industry-relevant D.Pharm and B.Pharm education through supportive faculty, modern laboratories, disciplined learning, and opportunities for academic and personal growth.
