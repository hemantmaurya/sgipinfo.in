About Us

Welcome to SGIP

We are passionate about grooming leaders who are not only thorough professionals but also good human beings with values and sanskars. We benchmark only against the best institutions around the world. Our faculty and senior team travel all over the globe to learn and imbibe the best practices so that we can give a solid foundation for learning. As part of this endeavour, we have modern classrooms that provide the most conducive atmosphere for dynamic and focused discussions, while the libraries at our campus are equipped with over 10,000 books, periodicals, national and international journals, CD-ROMS, covering all aspects of academic studies and research material. The hi-tech labs act as ideal training grounds for budding professionals that allow students to experiment and bring to practice what they have learnt in theory.

Our Popular Course

D.Pharm

The Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) is a two-year program affiliated with the Uttar Pradesh Board of Technical Education, Lucknow. Students build practical knowledge in pharmaceutical sciences, drug formulation, pharmacology, and pharmacy practice through classroom teaching and laboratory training.

Undergraduate Program

B.Pharm

The Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) is a four-year undergraduate program in Uttar Pradesh that develops a strong foundation in pharmaceutical sciences, medicines, pharmacology, and patient care. The course combines theory, practical laboratory work, seminars, and project-based learning.

With guidance from experienced faculty and access to modern learning facilities, students prepare for careers in hospitals, community pharmacies, pharmaceutical companies, quality control, research, and regulatory services.
