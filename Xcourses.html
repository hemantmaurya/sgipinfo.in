Diploma Program

D.Pharma

A 2-year diploma program affiliated with Uttar Pradesh Board of Technical Education, Lucknow. Covers pharmaceutical sciences, drug formulation, pharmacology, and pharmacy practice with hands-on training in our dedicated pharmaceutical lab. Prepares students for careers in pharmacies, hospitals, pharmaceutical industries, and regulatory affairs.
